## Mnemonics for the trickiest words

<Callout type="tip" title="Memory hooks">
  - **Abate** sounds like "a-bait." After you bait fish, the splashing **abates** (lessens).
  - **Acumen** sounds like "accumin'" sharpness. Sharp judgment.
  - **Belie** "be-lie." Her smile **belies** (lies about) her sadness.
  - **Censure** "censor" but for people. To formally rebuke.
  - **Hubris** rhymes with "loose-bris" (loose britches). Big ego, baggy pants.
  - **Insipid** "in-sip-id." A drink so bland you can barely sip it.
  - **Lurid** sounds like "lured." Lurid headlines lure clicks.
  - **Maudlin** "mod-lin." Modeline drinks too much wine, gets weepy.
  - **Pacifist** "Peace-i-fist." Anti-violence.
  - **Pious** "pie-us." Devout religious people sometimes hand out pie.
  - **Sanguine** "sang-win." Sang a winning song. Optimistic.
  - **Sycophant** "Sick-uh-fant." A flattering elephant who makes you sick of him.
  - **Tenuous** "ten-you-iss." A connection that's only ten percent there.
  - **Ubiquitous** "you-bik-witch-uss." Smartphones are with you everywhere.

## Common pitfalls

<BadFact source="every test-taker who didn't read the question" certainty="0%" title="MISCONCEPTION 1">
  Vindicate and vindictive mean the same thing.
</BadFact>

They don't. **Vindicate** = to clear someone of blame. **Vindictive** = wanting revenge. Opposite vibes.

<BadFact source="anyone who skipped the cards" certainty="0%" title="MISCONCEPTION 2">
  Sanguine means bloody and angry.
</BadFact>

The Latin root means blood, but the word itself means **cheerfully optimistic**. Don't be tricked.

<BadFact source="rushed final-exam takers" certainty="0%" title="MISCONCEPTION 3">
  Belie means "to confirm" or "to support."
</BadFact>

The opposite. **Belie** means **to give a false impression** of something. "Her smile belied her sadness" = her smile hid her sadness.

<BadFact source="confused vocab students everywhere" certainty="0%" title="MISCONCEPTION 4">
  Ingenuous means clever and inventive.
</BadFact>

That's **ingenious** (with an "i" in the middle). **Ingenuous** means **naive** or **innocent**.
